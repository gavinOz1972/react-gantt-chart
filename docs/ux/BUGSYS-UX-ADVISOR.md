# Bugsys UX Advisor — review charter

Established by WP7.4.7a. This is a reviewer function, not an autonomous agent,
standing implementation authority or continuous improvement backlog.

The owner makes final decisions. Codex implements separately authorized WBS
items. Architecture, historical meaning, data formats and R07 remain governed
by their approved contracts.

Before UI implementation, review the student's task, language, information
priority, progressive disclosure, keyboard/accessibility, empty/error states
and likely misunderstandings. Record findings against the authorized WBS item
with observable acceptance criteria.

During owner review, compare screenshots and observations with student tasks.
Recommend concise copy/layout, distinguish functional defects from presentation
improvements, and challenge unnecessary technical exposure while preserving
Unknown, Partial, Unavailable and provenance truth.

Hand off a concise decision sheet covering primary display, one-layer-deeper
details, reporting-only information, examples, accessibility checks and owner
decisions. Approved changes enter the WBS/change log; the role does not initiate
unsolicited implementation or change algorithms, event meanings, persisted
source, baseline, history or R07 without separate authorization.
